Fact of the Day ?

Canada's Bay of Fundy tide rises as high as a four-storey building, twice a day

At the head of the Bay of Fundy, between New Brunswick and Nova Scotia, the gap between low and high tide reaches 16 metres — Parks Canada compares it to a four-storey building, and it happens twice every day. The bay's funnel shape combines with a natural sloshing rhythm that matches the ocean tide. Further out in the bay the range is nearer 12 metres.

Parks Canada · Fundy National Park

A self-paced six-chapter course from the University of Helsinki explaining what AI actually is, what it can and cannot do, and how it affects everyday life. It states plainly that no maths or programming is required. Part 2 does recommend Python, so stick to Part 1.

Free · verified 6 Aug

About seven hours across four modules on what machine learning can realistically do, how AI projects work, and how to talk about AI with technical colleagues. Enrol for free; only the certificate costs money.

Free · verified 6 Aug

Free · verified 6 Aug

Learn by Doing

1

Rewrite something you already had to write

Take a real email or message you sent this week, paste it into a free chatbot and ask it to make it shorter, warmer or more formal. Comparing three versions of text you actually wrote teaches you more than any explanation will.

Try it this week
2

Make it explain something in your own terms

Pick something you half-understand — a pension letter, a medical leaflet — and ask for an explanation as if to someone with no background, then keep asking why. Check one fact it gives you against a real website, so you learn early that it can sound confident and still be wrong.

Try it this week
3

Use it as a planner for one small real task

Give it a concrete job you are facing this week — a week of dinners, a packing list — and refine the answer by saying what you did not like. Learning to say “no, shorter” is the most useful habit a beginner can build.

Try it this week

A Simple Way to Pace It

1

Week 1 — get comfortable

Create a free chatbot account and do the three things above, one per sitting, without worrying about how any of it works.

2

Week 2 — understand what AI is

Start Elements of AI Part 1 and aim for the first three chapters, in twenty or thirty minute sittings.

3

Week 3 — finish the foundations

Finish the remaining chapters of Part 1, and start AI For Everyone's first module if you have the appetite.

4

Week 4 — focus on today's tools

Work through Generative AI for Everyone, an hour or two spread across the week. It maps directly onto the chatbot you have been using for a month.
