Fact of the Day

The world’s biggest cable-car network isn’t at a ski resort — it’s a city bus service in Bolivia

La Paz sits in a valley so steep that trains and metros were never practical, so the city built upwards instead. Mi Teléférico runs 33 km of cable car across ten colour-coded lines and 32 stations, carrying ordinary commuters between La Paz and El Alto. Guinness World Records lists it as the largest public-transit cable-car system on Earth — the daily commute, by gondola.

Getting Started with AI

You do not need to be good at maths or coding to understand AI. Almost everything worth knowing at the start is about what these tools do, where they get things wrong, and how to ask them good questions — and you learn that by using them, not by reading about them. Pick one thing below and give it half an hour. That is genuinely enough to begin.

1

Free, made by the University of Helsinki, and built for people with no technical background at all. Around 30 hours in total, but you can do it in small pieces, and there is a certificate at the end.

Learn by Doing

1

Claude

Give it a real, small job rather than a test question. Ask it to explain something from school in a different way, or to help you plan a piece of homework. Then check whether it was right.

claude.ai · verified 29 Jul
2

ChatGPT

Try the same task in both and see where they disagree. Spotting the differences teaches you more than either answer on its own.

chatgpt.com · verified 29 Jul
3

Teachable Machine

Train an AI yourself in about ten minutes using your webcam — no code. It is the fastest way to see what “training a model” actually means, and why AI picks up bad habits from bad examples.

Google · verified 29 Jul

A Simple Way to Pace It

Week 1 — Just use it

Use Claude or ChatGPT for one real task every day. No reading, no courses. Notice when it gets something wrong.

Week 2 — Build one

Do Teachable Machine. Train it on something silly, then deliberately train it badly and watch what happens. That is bias, in about fifteen minutes.

Week 3 — Get the vocabulary

Start Elements of AI. Do a chapter or two. Now the words people use will have something real behind them.

Week 4 — See the bigger picture

Audit a few lessons of AI For Everyone. It is about how AI changes jobs and organisations, which is the part that will matter most to you.
