Fact of the Day ?

Peru grows more than 4,000 different kinds of potato

The International Potato Center says more than 4,000 varieties of native potato grow in the Andean highlands of Peru, Bolivia and Ecuador — in every shape and colour from cream to deep purple. Farmers picked them out over centuries to suit fields between 3,500 and 4,200 metres above sea level, where almost nothing else will grow.

International Potato Center · verified 31 Jul

You do not need any technical background to start with AI, and you do not need to spend anything. The three courses below are free to take, written for complete beginners, and each one is short. Start with the first, try one of the tools underneath while you go, and use the four-week plan at the bottom if you would rather have a shape to follow than pick your own pace.

Free Courses to Try

1

Elements of AI — Part 1: Introduction to AI

A free, self-paced course from the University of Helsinki explaining what AI actually is and what it can and cannot do. No maths and no programming. Usually taken over about six weeks. Stick to Part 1 — Part 2, “Building AI”, recommends Python and is not for absolute beginners.

University of Helsinki · verified 31 Jul
2

AI For Everyone

Andrew Ng’s non-technical introduction to what AI can do for you and your workplace, covering AI projects, teams and society. Free to enrol by choosing the audit option; about seven hours in total. Only the certificate costs money.

Generative AI for Everyone

A follow-on course on how tools like ChatGPT actually work and how to use them well. Free to enrol, beginner level, no AI or coding knowledge needed. Roughly six hours spread over three weeks at one to two hours a week.

DeepLearning.AI on Coursera · verified 31 Jul

A Simple Way to Pace It

1

Week 1 — get the vocabulary

Do the first two chapters of Elements of AI Part 1. Aim for about 30 minutes, three times in the week. The goal is simply that words like model, training and data stop being fuzzy.

2

Week 2 — use it every day

Finish Elements of AI Part 1 and put ChatGPT’s free plan to work on something real — drafting a message, planning a trip, summarising a long document. Noticing where it gets things wrong teaches you as much as where it gets them right.

3

Week 3 — see the wider picture

Work through AI For Everyone. It is about how AI is used by organisations rather than how it is built, so it fills in the part the first course deliberately leaves out.

4

Week 4 — play, then decide

Do Generative AI for Everyone and spend an hour trying image or audio tools on Hugging Face Spaces or Adobe Firefly. By the end of the month you will know which direction you actually want to go deeper in.
