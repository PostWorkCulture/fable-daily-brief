Free Courses to Try

1

Elements of AI

Free, from the University of Helsinki and MinnaLearn. Explicitly no maths and no programming required. Self-paced — the site no longer states an hour count. Stick to Part 1, “An Introduction to AI”; Part 2 does expect some Python.

elementsofai.com · verified 29 Jul
2

AI For Everyone

Free to enrol and audit, from Andrew Ng. Marked beginner level with no prior experience required, 7 hours across 4 modules. A paid certificate is optional — you don’t need it to do the course.

Coursera · verified 29 Jul
3

Generative AI For Everyone

Free to enrol and audit, also from Andrew Ng. States no prior knowledge of AI or coding needed, about 6 hours across 3 modules, paced as 1–2 hours a week for three weeks.

Coursera · verified 29 Jul

Learn by Doing

1

Just talk to one

The fastest way in. Free tier, no payment. Give it something real and small from your actual week — rewrite this awkward paragraph, summarise these notes, explain what this document is really saying. The trick is treating it like a colleague, not a search box.

claude.ai · free plan verified 29 Jul
2

Compare a second one

Ask both the same question and notice where they disagree. Seeing two answers side by side is the quickest way to stop treating any single answer as automatically correct.

chatgpt.com · verified 29 Jul
3

Train a model yourself, in the browser

Google’s Teachable Machine lets you train a model to recognise images, sounds or poses using your webcam. No coding, nothing to install. Ten minutes here makes “training data” stop being an abstract phrase.

Teachable Machine · verified 29 Jul

A Simple Way to Pace It

1

Week 1 — get the vocabulary

Do the first two modules of Elements of AI. The goal isn’t mastery, it’s that words like “model”, “training data” and “algorithm” stop sliding past you.

2

Week 2 — use one properly every day

Fifteen minutes a day with Claude or ChatGPT on real tasks. Keep a note of what worked and what produced nonsense — the failures teach you more than the successes.

3

Week 3 — watch AI For Everyone

Seven hours total, so an hour a night. This is the one that explains what AI can and can’t do for an organisation, which is the part most people get wrong.

4

Week 4 — build one tiny thing

Twenty minutes on Teachable Machine, then finish Generative AI For Everyone. By the end you will have trained a model and be able to explain the difference between generative AI and the rest — which is more than most people at work can do.
