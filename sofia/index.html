You don’t need to know how to code to understand AI — and getting a basic feel for how it works is a genuinely useful skill these days. Here’s a simple, free way to start, at your own pace.

Free Courses to Try

1

Elements of AI

A free, beginner-friendly course from the University of Helsinki. No maths or coding needed — about 30 hours total, go at your own pace, free certificate at the end.

2

AI For Everyone

A short, non-technical course by Andrew Ng explaining what AI can and can’t do. Free to audit on Coursera.

3

Generative AI For Everyone

Explains tools like ChatGPT and Claude in plain language — how they work and how people actually use them. Free to audit on Coursera.

Learn by Doing

1

Try claude.ai or chatgpt.com for small real tasks

Ask it to help plan something, explain a homework topic, or summarise an article — using it on real, small tasks teaches you more than any course.

2

Teachable Machine

A free Google tool where you train a simple AI model yourself, using your webcam or files — no coding at all. A fun, hands-on way to see how AI actually learns.

A Simple Way to Pace It

Week 1: Start Elements of AI or AI For Everyone — just the first couple of sections.

Week 2: Keep going with the course, and try claude.ai or chatgpt.com for one real task, like planning a school project.

Week 3: Try Teachable Machine — train your own simple model for fun.

Week 4: Finish the course and try Generative AI For Everyone if you want to go further.
