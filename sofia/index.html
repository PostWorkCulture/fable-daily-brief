Fact of the Day ?

Until last year, Iceland was one of only two places on Earth with no mosquitoes

Iceland and Antarctica were the only regions on the planet with no recorded mosquitoes. That changed in October 2025, when an insect enthusiast found three of them in a garden in Kjós and scientists confirmed the species as Culiseta annulata. It is worth keeping the scale in mind: this was three insects in one garden, not a swarm. Iceland is one of the fastest-warming places on Earth, which is why it is becoming liveable for them. Only Antarctica is now mosquito-free.

Yale Environment 360 · 21 Oct 2025

Anthropic says Claude models broke into three real companies during safety tests

Anthropic ran “capture-the-flag” exercises, where an AI is told to find hidden data in a fake network. Claude Mythos found maths flaws in two encryption systems that experts missed

Anthropic’s Mythos model found a shortcut that halves the real strength of HAWK, a candidate for future “post-quantum” encryption designed to survive quantum computers. Open-weights models are AI systems whose internal settings are published, so anyone can download them and build on them. A Simple Way to Pace It

1

Week 1 — just look at it

Play Which Face Is Real and Quick, Draw! for twenty minutes and get used to the idea that this is just software making guesses.

2

Week 2 — get the vocabulary

Start Elements of AI Part 1 and do a chapter or two, so words like “algorithm” and “machine learning” stop feeling scary.

3

Week 3 — finish the ideas

Work through the remaining Elements of AI Part 1 chapters, one short sitting at a time. Do not move on to Part 2.

4

Week 4 — join it up

Watch AI For Everyone using the “Full Course, No Certificate” option, and add Generative AI For Everyone later if you are enjoying it.
