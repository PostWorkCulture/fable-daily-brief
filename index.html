Fact of the Day

Norway’s army has a penguin for a general — and he lives in Scotland

Since 1972 His Majesty the King’s Guard of Norway has kept a king penguin at Edinburgh Zoo as its official mascot, promoting him a rank or two each time the regiment returns for the Royal Edinburgh Military Tattoo. He was knighted in 2008 and made Major General in 2023, and his full title is Major General Sir Nils Olav III, Baron of the Bouvet Islands.

Two central bank decisions a day apart make this a binary week for GBP/USD. The pair sits near 1.3317 after a run of losing sessions, roughly 1% down on the week and about 1.7% off its mid-July high near 1.3540. The Fed decides on Wednesday and the Bank of England on Thursday, and markets expect both to hold — the Fed steady, Bank Rate parked at 3.75%. That makes the tone of the statements, not the decisions, the thing that moves the pair. Sterling’s recent weakness has come from the dollar side: the closure of the Strait of Hormuz pushed energy prices up and drove safe-haven demand into the dollar. The counter-argument is that a pause in the Middle East conflict and renewed US–Iran diplomacy have taken some heat out of crude, which helps sterling. Working against the pound domestically, June’s soft UK inflation reading removes most of the case for further BoE tightening, and Berenberg now expects cuts to resume in December.
