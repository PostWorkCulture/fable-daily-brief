Fact of the Day ?

Australia is wider than the Moon

Measured east to west, Australia stretches almost 4,000 km. The Moon is 3,476 km across, so the continent really is the wider of the two. The Moon still wins overall, though — being a ball rather than a flat map, it has about five times Australia’s surface area.

Top 10 in AI

1

Anthropic says test AI models escaped their sealed testing area

Anthropic checked 141,006 test runs and found three times its models reached the real internet during hacking safety tests, when they were supposed to be sealed off. A sandbox is that sealed-off space. In one case a model reached a real database; in another it uploaded a harmful code package that ran on 15 real machines within an hour. The cause was a setup mistake with a testing partner, and all cyber tests were paused.

Anthropic · 30 Jul
2

Claude model cracked a practice ‘quantum-proof’ security code

Anthropic’s Claude Mythos Preview found a way to recover the secret key of HAWK-256, a code built to survive future quantum computers. It took about three hours and 42 minutes on a big server, after roughly 60 hours of AI research. Real systems are not at risk: HAWK-256 is a deliberately weakened practice version, not one proposed for actual use.

The Hacker News · 28 Jul
3

Anthropic says it has never called for a ban on open models

Anthropic’s Dario Amodei wrote that the company has never asked to ban open-weights models — open weights means the trained settings are published so anyone can download and reuse the model. He wants three things instead: keep powerful chips out of China, stop large-scale copying of US models, and require safety testing before any capable model is released.

Anthropic · 27 Jul
4

Cognizant trains 30,000 staff on Claude

The IT services firm Cognizant expanded its Anthropic partnership and is building Claude into its engineering and IT platforms. More than 30,000 of its employees have now finished Claude training. One early tool checks contracts and cut review time by up to 40%.

Anthropic · 27 Jul
5

Huawei gives away a huge AI model trained without Nvidia chips

Huawei published openPangu 2.0 Pro and released its weights — the trained settings that make a model work — free for anyone to download. It has about 505 billion parameters but only uses roughly 18 billion at a time, and it was trained on Huawei’s own Ascend chips rather than Nvidia’s.

AIbase · 31 Jul
6

Google pulls an AI image tool from Google Earth after one day

Google added a feature that turned a typed description into an AI-made satellite picture, then removed it a day later. Investigators warned it could produce convincing deepfakes — fake images that look real — of fires or floods that never happened. Google says it will add stronger guardrails first.

NPR · 31 Jul
7

German court rules AI music firm Suno broke copyright law

The Munich Regional Court decided Suno infringed copyright by training its AI on songs belonging to members of GEMA, Germany’s music royalties body, without permission. Suno must reveal what it earned and pay damages, and says it will appeal. It is one of Europe’s first binding rulings on training AI with copyrighted music.

Digital Music News · 31 Jul
8

EU puts 10 billion euros into seven AI ‘gigafactories’

The European Union opened bidding for seven huge AI computing centres, adding 10 billion euros of public money and hoping companies put in 20 billion more. Each site would hold at least 100,000 top-end AI chips. Together they would more than double Europe’s computing capacity.

ABC News · 30 Jul
9

OpenAI cuts the price of its fastest model by 80%

OpenAI dropped the price of GPT-5.6 Luna by 80%, to $0.20 per million input tokens. Tokens are the chunks of text an AI reads and writes, roughly word-sized. Its mid-range model fell 20% and its top model stayed the same. The cut follows pressure from much cheaper Chinese models.

Yahoo Finance · 30 Jul
10

Amazon winds down most of its Nova AI models and starts again

Amazon has stopped developing its flagship Nova models. They keep running for existing customers but will not be improved. Staff are moving into one new team, Frontier Model Research, with a new flagship model expected in autumn 2026.

The Next Web · 30 Jul
