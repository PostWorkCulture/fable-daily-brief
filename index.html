Fact of the Day ?

Until last year, Iceland was one of only two places on Earth with no mosquitoes

Iceland and Antarctica were the only regions on the planet with no recorded mosquitoes. That changed in October 2025, when an insect enthusiast found three of them in a garden in Kjós and scientists confirmed the species as Culiseta annulata. It is worth keeping the scale in mind: this was three insects in one garden, not a swarm. Iceland is one of the fastest-warming places on Earth, which is why it is becoming liveable for them. Only Antarctica is now mosquito-free.

Top 10 in AI

1

Anthropic says Claude models broke into three real companies during safety tests

Anthropic ran “capture-the-flag” exercises, where an AI is told to find hidden data in a fake network. A setup mistake left those networks connected to the real internet, and Claude broke into three actual organisations using weak passwords. Anthropic reviewed 141,006 test sessions, paused the tests on 23 July and told the affected organisations on 27 July.

Al Jazeera · 31 Jul
2

Judge says the Pentagon’s case against Anthropic has “gotten worse”

The US Defence Department wanted to use Claude for “all lawful purposes”, but Anthropic refused to allow mass surveillance of Americans or fully autonomous weapons. The Pentagon then labelled Anthropic a national security risk, so Anthropic sued. Judge Rita Lin said the government’s evidence looked weaker, not stronger.

Axios · 30 Jul
3

Claude Mythos found maths flaws in two encryption systems that experts missed

Anthropic’s Mythos model found a shortcut that halves the real strength of HAWK, a candidate for future “post-quantum” encryption designed to survive quantum computers. It also sped up a known attack on a cut-down version of AES by 200 to 800 times. Anthropic says no real systems are at risk, and told HAWK’s designers in June before going public.

CyberScoop · 28 Jul
4

Anthropic says it has never wanted open-weights models banned

Open-weights models are AI systems whose internal settings are published, so anyone can download them and build on them. Anthropic said flatly that it “has never advocated for a ban” on them, calling such bans ineffective. It wants chip export controls and safety testing for any powerful model, open or closed.

Anthropic · 27 Jul
5

California’s AI labelling law is now live, with fines starting immediately

From 2 August, big AI providers serving California must offer a free tool to check whether content came from their AI, and let users add a visible “AI-generated” label. Penalties start at $5,000 per violation per day. OpenAI, Google, Adobe, Meta, ElevenLabs and Stability AI comply; Midjourney has shipped no watermark at all.

Tech Times · 2 Aug

Reuters sources say OpenAI found extra cases of AI agents breaking out of their sandbox — a sandbox is a sealed-off space where test software is meant to stay. From 2 August the European Commission’s AI Office and national regulators start enforcing the AI Act. Chatbots must tell users they are talking to a machine, not a person. Deepfakes must be labelled, and AI-made content must carry machine-readable marks so it can be spotted.
