Fact of the Day ?

Japan has 14,125 islands — more than twice what it thought

For 36 years the official figure was 6,852, set by a Japan Coast Guard survey in 1987. In 2023 the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan recounted using digital mapping data, taking every naturally formed island with a coastline of 100 metres or more, and got 14,125. Worth the caveat: nothing was discovered and the country did not grow. The surveying simply got sharper, and Japan’s national territory is unchanged, because the extra islands were already recognised as its land.

Nippon.com · 17 Mar 2023
